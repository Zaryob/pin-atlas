Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Standard 14-contact XIAO: 11 GPIO + 3 power/ground contacts. nRF52840 BLE and internal antenna. The Sense variant adds an onboard LSM6DS3TR-C 6-axis IMU and PDM microphone. [b]

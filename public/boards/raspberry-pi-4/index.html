Cautions for this board

  • GPIO pins operate at 3.3 V and are not 5 V tolerant. Never apply 5 V to a GPIO pin; it can permanently damage the SoC. Source: [1]
  • A single GPIO pin can supply up to 16 mA; all GPIO pins combined can supply at most 50 mA. Source: [1]

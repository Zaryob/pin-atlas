Model-specific features and connection notes

  • 8 PIO state machines; 2 UART, 2 SPI, 2 I²C; USB 1.1. [a]
  • Board exposes 26 GPIOs and 3 external ADC inputs. GP29 is used for on-board measurements. [c]

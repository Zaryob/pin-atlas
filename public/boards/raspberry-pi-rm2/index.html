Raspberry Pi RM2 pinout

CYW43439 · Wi-Fi 4 + Bluetooth 5.2 · 21-pad SMT module

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

21 castellated SMT edge pads. Infineon CYW43439 radio IC; single-band 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n) and Bluetooth 5.2. Controlled over gSPI via the Pico SDK; includes 3 host-controlled GPIO expander pins (GPIO0-2).

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Infineon CYW43439 single-band (2.4 GHz) Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n) and Bluetooth 5.2 radio combo chip. Features a gSPI interface and 3 host-controlled GPIO expanders. [a]
  • 21 castellated SMT edge pads, shared 2.4 GHz onboard PCB antenna, low pin-count gSPI bus, and 3 GPIO expander pins. [a]

Arduino Micro pinout

ATmega32U4 · 16 MHz · 5 V I/O

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB at top. 34 side-header and 6 ICSP contacts; 24 independent GPIOs. This is a separate footprint from Nano. CIPO/SCK/SS/COPI occupy extra side contacts; ICSP repeats three of them. I²C uses D2/D3 and 12 ADC inputs share several digital pins. USB Serial and D0/D1 Serial1 are separate.

General usage notes

ATmega32U4 5 V I/O. Coordinate GPIO and peripheral modes; check the MCU datasheet for pin, port and total current limits. PWM is not a true analog output.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Flash capacity is the total; the bootloader occupies part of it. USB serial and the header UART are separate interfaces. [b]
  • ATmega32U4 provides native USB. SPI is on ICSP contacts; I²C uses D2/SDA and D3/SCL. [b]

General usage notes

3.3 V I/O; do not apply 5 V. Matrix choices need SDK pin routing and peripheral configuration. I²C requires open-drain operation and suitable pull-ups.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Standard 14-contact XIAO: 11 GPIO + 3 power/ground contacts. Houses ESP32-S3R8 with 8 MB Flash and 8 MB Octal PSRAM. The Sense add-on board provides an OV2640 camera, digital microphone, and microSD card slot. [b]

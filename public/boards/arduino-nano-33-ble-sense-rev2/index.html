Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2 pinout

nRF52840 · 64 MHz · 3.3 V I/O · BLE + Sensors

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB at top. 30 side contacts and 22 independent GPIOs; all digital pins support PWM. Warning: 3.3 V I/O only; NOT 5 V tolerant. Onboard sensors include BMI270 (6-axis IMU), BMM150 (3-axis magnetometer), LPS22HB (barometer), APDS-9960 (gesture/proximity/light), HS3003 (humidity/temp) and MP34DT06JTR (microphone). D1/RX and D0/TX are inverted relative to classic Nano.

General usage notes

3.3 V I/O logic level. nRF52840 pins are NOT 5 V tolerant; applying >3.3 V permanently damages the MCU. All GPIOs support hardware/software PWM and pin-change interrupts.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • NINA-B306/nRF52840: BLE, native USB and sensors. Rev2 IMU: BMI270 + BMM150; temperature/humidity: HS3003. GPIO is not 5 V tolerant. [a]

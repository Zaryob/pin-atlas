Arduino Leonardo pinout

ATmega32U4 · 16 MHz · 5 V I/O

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB at top. 32 UNO-format header and 6 ICSP contacts; 23 independent GPIOs. I²C uses D2/D3; SPI uses D14–D16 on ICSP. A4/A5 do not carry the UNO I²C signals. The 12 ADC inputs share six analog-header and six digital-header pins. USB Serial is separate from Serial1 on D0/D1.

General usage notes

ATmega32U4 5 V I/O. Coordinate GPIO and peripheral modes; check the MCU datasheet for pin, port and total current limits. PWM is not a true analog output.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Flash capacity is the total; the bootloader occupies part of it. USB serial and the header UART are separate interfaces. [a]
  • I²C: D2/D3; SPI: ICSP header. Do not use the UNO D11–D13 SPI mapping. USB is native to ATmega32U4. [d]

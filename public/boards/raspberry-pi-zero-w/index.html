Raspberry Pi Zero W pinout

J8 · 40-pin footprint (unpopulated) · BCM2835 alternate functions

Zero W ships with an unpopulated 40-pin GPIO header footprint (the WH model has the header fitted). Pin numbers follow the standard 40-pin layout. The mini HDMI and two micro USB ports are not header pins. Alternate functions come from the BCM2835 table.

Cautions for this board

  • GPIO pins operate at 3.3 V and are not 5 V tolerant. Never apply 5 V to a GPIO pin; it can permanently damage the SoC. Source: [1]
  • A single GPIO pin can supply up to 16 mA; all GPIO pins combined can supply at most 50 mA. Source: [1]
  • The 40-pin header is unpopulated on this model. Solder a 2 × 20, 2.54 mm pin header for GPIO access, or use the model with the header fitted. Source: [2] [3]
  • Alternate functions come from the BCM2835 ARM Peripherals GPIO table (§6.2). Raspberry Pi documentation notes that this document contains known errors; selections shown as reserved in the table are omitted. Source: [4] [5] [6]
  • BCM2835/BCM2836/BCM2837/RP3A0 GPIO levels: input low at most 0.9 V, input high at least 1.6 V (hysteresis enabled). Default drive strength 8 mA, maximum 16 mA; internal pull-up/pull-down 50–65 kΩ. Source: [7]

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • 40-pin header uses 3.3 V GPIO. Physical pin numbers differ from BCM GPIO numbers. Pins 27/28 are the HAT ID EEPROM bus. The header footprint ships unpopulated (fitted on the WH model). [d] [b] [e]

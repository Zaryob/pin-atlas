BeagleBone Black pinout

TI AM3358 · 1 GHz · 3.3 V I/O

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

Ethernet and DC jack at top. Two 46-pin P8 and P9 expansion headers; 67 GPIO-capable 3.3 V contacts (30 of them used by the on-board eMMC/HDMI by default; P9.41 and P9.42 each tie two processor balls) and 7 analog inputs (AIN0–AIN6). TI AM3358 1 GHz Cortex-A8 and dual 200 MHz PRU cores. CAUTION: Analog inputs (AIN) are 1.8 V maximum! Voltages above 1.8 V destroy the ADC. Digital pins are 3.3 V only and not 5 V tolerant.

Cautions for this board

  • Do not apply voltage to any I/O pin while the board is unpowered, and drive no pin until SYS_RESETn has gone high (the VDD_3V3B rail is up); otherwise the processor is damaged. Header signals are 3.3 V: 5 V logic levels damage the board. Source: [1] [2]
  • Of the 67 GPIO-capable header contacts, 30 are used by on-board devices by default: P8.3–6 and P8.20–25 by the eMMC, P8.27–46 by the HDMI framer. Source: [3] [4]

General usage notes

3.3 V I/O logic level. TI AM3358 pins are NOT 5 V tolerant; voltages above 3.3 V damage the SoC. Pinmux modes (Mode 0–7) are configured via Device Tree overlays. Source: [1]

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • P8/P9 pins may be shared with HDMI and eMMC. Device Tree/pinmux configuration is required. Do not apply 3.3 V to analog inputs. [b]

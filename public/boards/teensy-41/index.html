Teensy 4.1 pinout

NXP i.MX RT1062 · 600 MHz · 3.3 V I/O

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB at top. 48 DIP edge pins (L.1–L.24 and R.1–R.24), 5-pin USB Host header, 6-pin Ethernet header and Micro SD socket; 48 independent GPIOs (0–47; the bottom memory pads 48–54 are not mapped). NXP i.MX RT1062 600 MHz Cortex-M7 processor. Warning: 3.3 V I/O only; NOT 5 V tolerant (except VIN and VUSB). All digital pins support interrupts.

General usage notes

3.3 V I/O logic level. Pins are NOT 5 V tolerant; voltages above 3.3 V will damage the processor. All GPIO pins support hardware interrupts. Source: [1]

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Top 256 KB of Flash is reserved for EEPROM emulation and restore code. Extra RAM/Flash footprints may be empty. [a]

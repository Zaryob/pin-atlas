Cautions for this board

  • D11–D18 are solder pads on the back of the board. The map shows them as a separate connection group. Source: [1]
  • RP2350-E9 (A2 stepping): a Bank 0 GPIO configured as an input can be held at about 2.2 V by pad leakage while its voltage is in the undefined logic region; the internal pull-down is too weak to overcome it. Pull or drive the line low with 8.2 kΩ or less; the pull-up still works. Source: [2]

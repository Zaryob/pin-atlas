General usage notes

GPIO pad in this package. Configure alternate functions, analog mode and peripherals in firmware; consult the manufacturer datasheet for electrical limits.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • v3.x board has a PC13 LED and PA0 button. Optional SPI Flash is separate from internal program Flash and is not fitted on every board. [c] [d]

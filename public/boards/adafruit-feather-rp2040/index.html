Model-specific features and connection notes

  • 8 PIO state machines; 2 UART, 2 SPI, 2 I²C; USB 1.1. [a]
  • STEMMA QT uses the board I²C bus. 133 MHz is the chip limit; the product guide also lists approximately 125 MHz operation. [b]

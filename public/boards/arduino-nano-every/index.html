Arduino Nano Every pinout

ATmega4809 · 16 MHz · 5 V I/O

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB at top. 30 side contacts and 22 independent GPIOs; A6/A7 also support digital I/O. Arduino PWM uses D3/D5/D6/D9/D10; D11 has no Arduino PWM support. A4 and A5 connect PF2/PA2 and PF3/PA3 respectively; coordinate I²C and ADC/GPIO drivers. Underside SAMD11/UPDI debug pads are outside this map.

General usage notes

5 V logic. Arduino's board guidance specifies 20 mA per I/O; port and total MCU current limits also apply. PWM is not a true analog voltage output. Coordinate timer and peripheral use in firmware.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • ATmega4809: 48 KB Flash, 6 KB SRAM and 256 B EEPROM. USB bridge is SAMD11; it does not use the Nano Classic AVR register map. [a]

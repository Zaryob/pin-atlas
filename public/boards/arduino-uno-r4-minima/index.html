Arduino UNO R4 Minima pinout

Renesas RA4M1 · 48 MHz · 5 V I/O

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB at top. 32 shared UNO contacts and 6 SPI contacts; 20 independent 5 V GPIOs. The first power-header contact is BOOT. A0 provides a true DAC; CAN0 TX/RX uses D4/D5 and needs an external CAN transceiver. Observe the 8 mA per-pin and 60 mA total limits. The SWD/debug header is outside this view.

General usage notes

RA4M1 5 V I/O. Observe 8 mA per-pin and 60 mA total limits. CAN needs an external transceiver. Configure timers and peripherals for the selected R4 model.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • RA4M1: 12-bit DAC, RTC and CAN controller. A CAN physical bus connection requires an external transceiver. [a]

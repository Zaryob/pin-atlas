ATmega328P · QFN/MLF-28 pinout

QFN/MLF-28 · bare MCU · up to 20 MHz

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

Bare ATmega328P package, not Arduino headers or Arduino sketch pin numbers. The 23 GPIO-capable pins include PC6 subject to the reset fuse and PB6/PB7 subject to clock configuration. ADC6/ADC7 exist only on 32-pin packages and are analog-only. QFN center GND pads are outside the perimeter count. Select supply and clock speed for the datasheet's speed grade.

General usage notes

Bare-MCU port bit. Configure I/O mode, peripheral and any required fuses; check voltage, current and clock limits in the MCU datasheet.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • 20 MHz requires 4.5–5.5 V; use the datasheet speed grade at lower voltages. [a]

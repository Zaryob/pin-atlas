NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano pinout

J12 · P3768 carrier · 40-pin expansion header

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

J12 on the NVIDIA carrier board; excludes the 260-pin module connector and third-party carrier boards. Signals use 3.3 V levels. Peripherals require Jetson-IO/pinmux configuration; this plan does not configure the device. Function names are carrier signals, not Linux bus numbers.

Arduino Nano ESP32 pinout

ESP32-S3 (NORA-W106) · 240 MHz · 3.3 V I/O · Wi-Fi + BLE

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB-C at top. 30 side contacts and 24 independent GPIOs. 3.3 V I/O logic level (NOT 5 V tolerant). B0 (GPIO46) and B1 (GPIO0) are strapping/boot pins. All GPIOs support PWM and interrupts.

Cautions for this board

  • The ESP32-S3 strapping pins are GPIO0, GPIO3, GPIO45 and GPIO46: B1 (GPIO0, green LED), A2 (GPIO3), the blue LED (GPIO45, not on the headers) and B0 (GPIO46, red LED). Their levels at reset select boot and hardware options; make sure external circuits do not force them during reset. Source: [1] [2]

General usage notes

3.3 V I/O level. ESP32-S3 pins are NOT 5 V tolerant; voltages above 3.3 V will damage the chip.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • u-blox NORA-W106 / ESP32-S3. Arduino D labels differ from ESP32 GPIO numbers; match Tools > Pin Numbering to your code. [b]

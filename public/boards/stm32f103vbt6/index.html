STM32F103VBT6 pinout

LQFP-100 · Cortex-M3 · 72 MHz

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

ST DS5319 and RM0008 specify 100 package pins and 80 GPIOs. OSC_IN and OSC_OUT are dedicated to the HSE oscillator. PD0 and PD1 are on pins 81 and 82. Each AFIO remap setting applies to all signals of the peripheral. Verify register settings and simultaneous-use constraints separately from the wiring plan. Do not assume 5 V tolerance in analog mode.

General usage notes

Configure the GPIO mode and any required AFIO remap setting. Peripherals sharing this pin cannot be used simultaneously.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Clock is the MCU maximum. Configure PLL, supply and Flash wait states accordingly. RAM banks do not have identical DMA accessibility. [b]

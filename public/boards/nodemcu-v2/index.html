General usage notes

3.3 V I/O; do not apply 5 V. Matrix choices need SDK pin routing and peripheral configuration. I²C requires open-drain operation and suitable pull-ups.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Houses an ESP-12E/F module with 4 MB internal SPI Flash. SDIO pins (GPIO6–GPIO11) connect directly to this flash and are reserved. Analog pin A0 includes an onboard 220 kΩ / 100 kΩ divider for a 0–3.3 V range. [b] [c]

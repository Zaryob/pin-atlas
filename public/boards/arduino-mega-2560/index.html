Arduino Mega 2560 Rev3 pinout

ATmega2560 · 70 GPIO · 16 analog inputs

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

USB at top. 86 outer-header and 6 main-MCU ICSP contacts; 70 independent GPIOs. SPI uses D50–D53; I²C uses D20/D21 and the duplicate SDA/SCL contacts. D0/D1 have no Arduino PWM support. On the end header, even GPIOs occupy the row nearer USB, odd GPIOs the outer row. USB bridge programming headers are outside this map.

General usage notes

5 V logic. Arduino's board guidance specifies 20 mA per I/O; port and total MCU current limits also apply. PWM is not a true analog voltage output. Coordinate timer and peripheral use in firmware.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • Flash capacity is the total; the bootloader occupies part of it. USB serial and the header UART are separate interfaces. [b]
  • 4 hardware UARTs; I²C on D20/SDA and D21/SCL. SPI: D50/MISO, D51/MOSI, D52/SCK, D53/SS and duplicated ICSP nets. [b]

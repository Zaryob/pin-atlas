ATmega2560 · TQFP-100 pinout

TQFP-100 · bare MCU · up to 16 MHz

Open in the interactive atlas →See the pin map, choose functions and save a wiring plan.Türkçe

100-pin TQFP ATmega2560: 86 GPIOs and 16 ADC inputs. These are package pins, not Mega D0–D69; they include signals absent from the board headers. The 64-pin ATmega2561 and CBGA use different packages. PF4–PF7 share JTAG. Simultaneous use depends on peripheral and fuse configuration.

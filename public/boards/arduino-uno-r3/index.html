USB at top. 32 main-header contacts and 6 main-MCU ICSP contacts; 20 independent GPIOs. SDA/SCL share A4/A5; ICSP signals share D11–D13. Functions cover Arduino AVR GPIO, ADC, PWM, SPI, I²C, UART and external interrupts. The USB bridge programming header is outside this map.

General usage notes

5 V logic. Arduino's board guidance specifies 20 mA per I/O; port and total MCU current limits also apply. PWM is not a true analog voltage output. Coordinate timer and peripheral use in firmware.

Model-specific features and connection notes

  • 20 MHz requires 4.5–5.5 V; use the datasheet speed grade at lower voltages. [a]
  • Flash capacity is the total; the bootloader occupies part of it. USB serial and the header UART are separate interfaces. [b]
